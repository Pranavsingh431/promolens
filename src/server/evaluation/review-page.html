How this works

  1. Enter your anonymous reviewer code. Your work is saved to your own file and you can resume any time.
  2. Part A, find moments. Read a whole episode and mark the clips you would post, 20 to 60 seconds each. Do this before Part B.
  3. Part B, rate clips. Rate clips one at a time as someone who has never seen the show. Judge only what is shown.

A clip can only be kept if the hook, the ending, and the clarity are all rated 2 or higher.
